\begin{abstract}
While deep learning has significantly improved automated crop disease detection, most current systems function as inscrutable "black boxes," limiting farmer trust and adoption. This paper presents ExplainableCropAI, a novel framework that integrates accurate paddy disease detection with multi-level explanations and context-aware recommendations. Our approach combines visual evidence highlighting (Grad-CAM), uncertainty quantification (Monte Carlo Dropout), and domain-specific natural language explanations to provide transparent and actionable insights. We demonstrate that this explainable system achieves 97.2\% disease classification accuracy while generating faithful visual explanations that localize disease regions with 72\% intersection over union (IoU) with ground truth annotations. Through a controlled user study with 45 agricultural stakeholders, we establish that explanations significantly improve trust (+53\%) and treatment decision accuracy (+29\%) compared to unexplained predictions, with the strongest benefits for users with less domain expertise. The system's context-aware recommendation engine generates treatment and prevention suggestions rated highly by agricultural experts for appropriateness (4.6/5) and specificity (4.3/5). These findings demonstrate that explainability can transform deep learning from a black-box prediction tool into an assistive technology that augments farmer decision-making without sacrificing accuracy—a critical advancement for responsible AI deployment in agriculture.
\end{abstract}
